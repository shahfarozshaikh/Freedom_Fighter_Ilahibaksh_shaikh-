Biography

The life and journey of Ilahibaksh Mohammedsahab Shaikh

Early Life

Late Ilahibaksh Mohammedsahab Shaikh was born in 1920 in Solapur, Maharashtra. He lived a life guided by courage, honesty, service and strong moral values.

Service to Society

Alongside his contribution to the freedom movement, he worked for social unity, harmony and the welfare and upliftment of weaker sections of society.

A Life of Values

His life reflected patriotism, integrity, selflessness and dedication to the welfare of society and the nation.

Passing & Legacy

He passed away in 2005, leaving behind a legacy of patriotism, social service, integrity and memories that continue to be cherished by his family and future generations.

Freedom Struggle

His contribution towards India's independence

1920 — Birth

Born in Solapur, Maharashtra.

From 1940 — Nationalist Work

He became associated with the Congress organisation and nationalist activities.

1942 — Quit India Movement

He actively participated in the Quit India Movement and contributed to the freedom struggle.

Underground Activities

He wrote, distributed and pasted bulletins and continued supporting the movement despite police raids, including work connected with the Gulbarga district.

Legacy of Service

His contribution was remembered not only for the freedom struggle but also for social service and community welfare.
